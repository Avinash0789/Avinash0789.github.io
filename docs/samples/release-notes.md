# Release notes – Acme Agency Manager 4.2

!!! info "Sample document"
    Shows how I write customer-facing release notes.

**Release date:** 14 September 2026

## Highlights

This release makes it faster to renew policies and adds two-way email sync, so your client conversations stay in one place.

## New features

### Bulk policy renewals

You can now renew up to 200 policies at once.

1. Go to **Policies > Renewals**.
2. Select the policies you want to renew.
3. Select **Renew selected** and review the summary.

Each client receives their renewal documents by email, and the activity is logged on their profile.

### Two-way email sync for Outlook

Emails you send and receive in Outlook now appear on the matching client's timeline automatically. To turn it on, go to **Settings > Integrations > Outlook** and select **Connect**.

## Improvements

- **Faster search** – client search results now load up to 3× faster for agencies with more than 50,000 records.
- **Clearer commission reports** – reports now show carrier name and policy type in separate columns, making them easier to filter in Excel.
- **Mobile app** – you can now upload documents from your phone's camera directly to a client profile.

## Fixed issues

- Fixed an issue where renewal reminders were sent twice to some clients.
- Fixed incorrect time zones on scheduled tasks for agencies outside the US.
- Fixed a display issue where long carrier names were cut off on the policy summary page.

## Coming soon
